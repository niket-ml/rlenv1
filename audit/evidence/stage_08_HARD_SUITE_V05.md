# Stage 8 — v0.5 construct-validity and local-control audit

Date: 2026-09-07

Decision: **local construction gate passed; paid model-difficulty gate remains closed**.

## Structural pivot

v0.4 is preserved unchanged as a documented no-go. Its main lessons were:

- near-ceiling quantitative performance did not create enough separation;
- one process-confounding family explained 43.6% of the Sol–GPT-5.2 gap;
- intervention selection did not reliably produce downstream containment or
  belief revision; and
- disconnected question/remedy pairs did not represent one commercial decision.

v0.5 therefore uses one persistent diligence chain. The same locked predictor,
claim ledger, prospective plan, validation reveal, resource choice, and final
decision persist across ten milestones.

## Internal multi-role review

The MVP has no external reviewers. The design was challenged sequentially from
the following professional perspectives. This is a documented self-review, not
independent validation.

| Role | Principal objection | Resolution in the build |
|---|---|---|
| IBD clinical lead | Endpoint and drug transfer could be reduced to generic caution | Endpoint sources, reviewer disagreement, timepoint, intended drug, and matched bridge evidence change claim admissibility |
| Biostatistician | AUC arithmetic could dominate again | Patient-level uncertainty, calibration, threshold utility, prospective rules, and conditional claim states share the score |
| Transcriptomics specialist | Platform language could be decorative | Agent receives feature contract, platform registry, transformation fit scope, outcome access, and a leakage-safe counterfactual rerun |
| Data curator | Identity failure could be a hidden trick | Reported identifiers, fingerprint groups, visits, and manifest completeness are visible; source crosswalk is a costed remedy |
| Validation engineer | Commitment could be narrative only | Predictor, plan, and analysis files are hashed; mutations are rejected before private evidence access |
| Commercial diligence lead | The episode could lack a real investment action | The terminal choice and single costed resource are tied to a predeclared intended-use gate |
| Experimental-design lead | Correct resource could be a memorized ID | Resources receive graded utility; effects include resolution, partial information, second blockers, ineffectiveness, and reassurance traps |
| Benchmark red team | Schemas or universal abstention could be shortcuts | Three compact action records, symmetric clean/irreducible cases, evidence-path checks, alternative methods, and four universal-policy controls |

## Exact local evidence

The generated control artifact is
`artifacts/diagnostics/hard_suite_v05_controls.json`.

- Development states: 6.
- Separately sealed held-out states: 6.
- Astra exposure: 0.
- Reference score: 100 on every state.
- Alternative expert-equivalent method score: 100 on every state.
- Deliberately mediocre scores: 34.865–50.600.
- Universal-policy mean scores:
  - always advance: 39.246;
  - always stop: 39.246;
  - always abstain: 40.096;
  - always request more data: 41.369.
- Single early identity-status error: 98; later validation and recovery remain
  fully scoreable.
- Pre-reveal outcome/private-answer paths: none.
- Committed predictor mutation: rejected.

Controlled scientific transitions include:

- clean case initial AUC 0.709, interval 0.621–0.785, conditional advance with
  no unnecessary resource;
- identity case AUC 0.862 but initial claims blocked until source reconciliation;
- endpoint case reviewer disagreement 0.155, removed by adjudication, while
  remaining evidence still supports insufficiency;
- leakage case apparent AUC 0.987 falling to 0.575 under the safe rerun, leading
  to stop;
- transfer case matched-bridge AUC 0.846 after jointly correcting endpoint,
  drug, and platform relevance; and
- underpowered case follow-up AUC 0.618 with interval 0.471–0.766, correctly
  remaining insufficient.

## Threat assessment

| Threat | Current control | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|
| Memorized literature | Controlled case instances and authentic/control separation | Authentic GEO biology remains potentially memorized |
| Hidden-answer guessing | Answerability ledger and observable source evidence | Model may infer generator regularities after repeated development exposure |
| Schema difficulty | Compact schemas, alternate-method control, evidence-based partial credit | Long episodes can still produce execution failures |
| Universal caution | Clean progression case and no-resource optimum | Models may overuse conditional language while still selecting correct actions |
| Numerical ceiling | Quantitative work is distributed across state transitions | Only model calibration can establish actual ceiling |
| Synthetic artificiality | Cohort- and process-shaped artifacts plus authentic GEO anchor | Controlled biology is not a substitute for external clinical review |
| One-family domination | Ten bounded milestones and six causal states | Gap-share cannot be measured before model runs |

## Gate status

Passed locally:

- configuration and partition isolation;
- expert-equivalent and alternative-workflow replay;
- deliberately mediocre separation;
- universal policy failure;
- graceful partial credit;
- immutable commitments and resource choices;
- answer-leakage scan;
- heterogeneous intervention effects; and
- deterministic private recomputation.

Still blocked:

- any claim that v0.5 separates Sol, GPT-5.4, and GPT-5.2;
- any stable model ranking;
- any held-out model evaluation;
- any Astra call; and
- any claim of external expert endorsement.

The next authorized empirical step, when deliberately approved, is the frozen
18-episode development pilot under the exact $20 incremental cap. A ceiling or
floor produces another explicit no-go rather than a leaderboard.
