## Locked initial assessment

The packet supports planning a patient-level assessment of sponsor predictions, not established predictive performance. There are 112 fingerprint clusters, 122 biopsy samples and ten repeated clusters. Supplied records support adult status, baseline timing and colonic mucosa/HTA2 sampling across three sites, but do not independently document severity, first infliximab exposure or the full eligibility history. Two endpoint records have unresolved source discordance; observed days are inside the stated window. Source concordance does not itself establish the clinical validity of response.

Configuration declares a training reference, while membership includes training and validation samples in reference fitting. The illustrative preprocessing uses that inclusion flag. Endpoint import precedes membership resolution, reference fitting and export in the execution log. These observations justify an execution audit; they do not prove that outcomes entered a calculation. Sponsor AUC 1.000 is unverified. Three reproduction examples lack sufficient inputs, tolerances and execution provenance to establish reproducibility.

Validation-feature fitting can compromise an intended training-only evaluation; locked model weights do not establish locked preprocessing. Pooled normalization is not automatically supervised outcome leakage, nor proof that it caused perfect discrimination. Plausible alternatives are inaccurate membership, stale configuration, an illustrative script differing from execution, linkage or endpoint errors, duplicated patients, cohort/batch effects and undocumented export production. Legitimately strong discrimination remains possible. Batch B-03 and two disputed endpoints do not alone prove a broad batch/label failure.

A primary analysis should choose an outcome-independent biopsy rule and patient-respecting uncertainty before reveal. Probability averaging and a consistently selected biopsy are defensible; treating biopsies as independent patients is not. Eligibility should follow documented fields and acknowledge missing history. Either a predeclared source hierarchy with endpoint sensitivity analysis, or justified exclusion of unresolved records, may be valid. Commit AUC/uncertainty, Brier, ECE bins and utility at 0.5 against the supplied criteria. Utility is statistical evidence, not demonstrated clinical benefit without a specified action and consequences. Site/batch and biopsy checks can be useful. Outcome-driven exclusions, threshold tuning or best-rule selection after reveal undermine commitment.

Sponsor-export and executable-artifact performance must be attributed to their respective prediction sets. Success on one does not validate the other.

X31 is strongly motivated when actual reference membership, execution or reproducibility affects the decision; usefulness depends on its generation and link to the export. X24 is defensible for endpoint-source uncertainty. X17 can address unresolved identity/chronology, although keys appear comparatively consistent. X46 addresses matched external performance of an identified predictor, not original execution. X58 addresses precision after validity issues. X63 is advisory, not new evidence. No purchase is defensible for a resolved bounded hold or when no catalogue evidence addresses the relevant question. Neither X31 nor any purchase is mandatory from the starting evidence.

The revised README names no defect, X31 or desired decision. The records themselves expose the reference-fit conflict: discovering it is evidence-based inference. The catalogue suggests a relevant resource without revealing its result.

The proposed manifest's statement of separate generations is a substantive provenance assertion, not independently authenticated by other supplied records. A weights digest and an artifact-byte digest over different objects do not establish historical relationship. Auditing them separately is reasonable but cannot empirically authenticate the relationship. Coefficients and executable inputs are initially unavailable. Later disclosure must clearly identify the generation of every preprocessing/reproduction record, return and recommendation. Otherwise material scientific ambiguity remains. The packet can support an audit of the export and a separate assessment of executable evidence, not transferring validity merely because intended use is shared.

Reasonable specialists may disagree about aggregation, endpoint precedence/exclusions, interval methods, materiality of pooled normalization, highest-value resource, and bounded stop versus investigation. They should agree that AUC alone is insufficient, the documentation conflict does not prove its causal effect, and evidence transfer between generations requires a demonstrated connection. No unique purchase or final decision follows from the starting packet.
