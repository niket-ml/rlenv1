# UC-Bench Case 2 readiness report

## Verdict

**Not ready to freeze or spend.** Case 2 is scientifically useful and demonstrably tractable, but a direct RC6 port would currently force a false choice between two unfair graders:

1. the old grader privately hard-codes PAUSE/STOP; or
2. the RC6 criteria-driven grader can allow CONTINUE after an agent chooses a weak site criterion just above chance.

The public intended-use contract contains pooled AUC, AUC-lower-bound, Brier, ECE and net-benefit gates, but no rule connecting context instability to advancement. This is a genuine construct-validity gap, not a reason to redesign the case or make it harder. A second, smaller adapter is required because Case 2 starts with 96 provisional fingerprint clusters and later resolves to 95 canonical people; copying Case 1's identity declaration would leak and erase the investigation.

No model call was made, no release was frozen, no Case 2 scientific byte was changed, and accepted Case 1 RC6 still validates at release digest `a1795f1be20b02bb02d86516a664a3d62aed7d6ca716bdd45cb277b4c1ea6d19`.

## 3. What must be replaced or adapted

- **Replace the private final-disposition whitelist.** Grade whether the final decision follows a publicly disclosed, prospectively committed and professionally defensible context standard—not whether it equals one hidden narrative.
- **Add a Case 2 identity adapter.** The pre-purchase mapping must be described as provisional. It may bind a defensible working analysis unit, but must not declare `fingerprint_cluster` canonical. X17 must remain capable of changing 96 provisional units into 95 adjudicated people.
- **Use RC6's one-source semantics pattern.** One Case 2 public recomputation/semantic engine must feed both local validation and hidden grading for resource relevance, returned result, materiality and effect.
- **Use RC6 committed-cohort and causal grading architecture, not its Case 1 constants.** Preserve valid upstream credit when a downstream artifact or decision fails.
- **Use RC6 request lifecycle, identity, replay, reporting and cell-local provider containment.** These host-only pieces have already been imported into the unfrozen development adapter.
