## Locked rule and exact repair

Before implementation or replay, the policy and ten-case metamorphic matrix were
locked. Optional diagnostic work may be absent, valid, invalid, malformed, or use
another sensitivity convention without changing an independently valid required
property. It becomes causal only if it mutates or conflicts with required evidence,
or a disclosed machine-readable field expressly uses that sub-result for a decision.
Required discrimination uncertainty remains strict.

The smallest repair is a separate development scorer. It:

1. derives optionality from public contract metadata and the criterion property's
   public non-vacuity rule—not from a metric, model, trajectory or score;
2. independently grades the original optional interval and keeps any fault visible;
3. recomputes strict science on a disposable coherent view with only the unrelied
   optional interval omitted;
4. never changes the preserved workspace or submission; and
5. retains lifecycle-owned completion and reliability.

The predecessor scorer's incidental in-memory uncertainty-alias mutation was also
contained by passing a deep copy. A macOS `/var` → `/private/var` path-alias issue in
the disposable-workspace guard was found by replay and fixed generically by resolving
the temporary root before containment checks.

## Optional-field and authority audit

The fresh agent-visible contract contains **80** optional, `required: false`,
diagnostic-only, or dynamic optional entries: 61 are diagnostic-only and 19 are
conditionally causal. The audit traces each field to its sole scientific authority
and records whether presence could ever lower strict credit.

The only legitimate strict paths are:

- a supplied optional selector actually changes required scope;
- an optional assertion conflicts with a required artifact or hash;
- an optional calculation is expressly referenced by a disclosed machine field; or
- a public property rule makes that information required.

Free text never affects strict scoring. The complete field inventory and the
property-level authority table are in the linked machine-readable and Markdown
