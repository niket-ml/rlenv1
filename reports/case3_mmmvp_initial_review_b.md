A clean executable return cannot retroactively validate original preprocessing or original evidence.
- Prospective commitment: Establish what was committed before outcome reveal, using execution timestamps with clock offsets accounted for. Resource relevance, eligibility rules, analysis choices, and interpretation rules must be justified without knowledge of which sealed return retains signal.
- Conditional quantitative validity: Once a return is obtained, independently reproduce consequential numerical claims from saved raw artifacts. Aggregation and appropriately clustered or weighted repeated-row analyses are acceptable when their estimands and uncertainty respect dependence. Missing or disputed endpoints cannot silently become responses or nonresponses.
- Decision validity: Ineligible original evidence cannot support advancement. A supported no-purchase hold can complete bounded current-use diligence after the required investigation. It cannot establish clean recovery. X31 with collapse requires abandoning the current claim; retained signal can support independent validation or a cautious hold because external gates remain unresolved.

Defensible alternatives include purchasing a resource that addresses an identified uncertainty, or purchasing none when the investigated evidence supports holding. Resource credit concerns its relevance before results, not subsequent improvement. An otherwise complete, evidence-supported constant-hold policy must remain eligible to pass.

Nearest-invalid workflows include counting dependent rows as independent patients; outcome-driven identity or endpoint selection; validation-contaminated normalization; substituting executable predictions for the original export during reconstruction; post-reveal analysis changes represented as commitments; unsupported clean-performance claims; and treating a generic hold as completed investigation.

Partial credit must be property-local. Correct eligibility work survives an unfinished pipeline investigation; correct provenance work survives an arithmetic error. An invalid calculation loses its quantitative credit and any decision credit that relies on it, without erasing unrelated work. Return-dependent calculation properties are inapplicable to a legitimate no-purchase mission, but unsupported claims about such calculations fail. Strict completion requires every applicable consequential property and a valid final submission.

Reward-hack controls should check saved evidence rather than prose resemblance. Restrict exact matching to disclosed machine fields. Confirm invariance under consistent identifier renaming and row reordering; challenge dependence handling with repeated rows; and verify appropriate sensitivity to changed fit membership, normalization inputs, hashes, endpoint windows, source conflicts, and clock offsets. Both sealed returns must receive identical ex-ante relevance scoring. Belief reports remain diagnostic.

The main unresolved ambiguity is what evidence establishes the original export’s generation and preprocessing provenance. The executable model’s provenance does not answer that question. Implementation must explicitly distinguish established facts, missing evidence, and unavailable reconstruction using the supplied materials. If original provenance cannot be established, the appropriate audit result is uncertainty or ineligibility—not an inferred history. Likewise, schema fields alone do not establish adult status or treatment timing unless their documented meanings or supplied evidence do so.
