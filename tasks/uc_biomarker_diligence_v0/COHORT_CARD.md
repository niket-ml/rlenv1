Development expression files are cohort-native normalized public GEO series
matrices collapsed to unambiguous gene symbols. Do not compare their absolute
intensity scales directly. The sealed transfer cohort is on a third platform
and changes both drug and endpoint. Its features and outcomes remain private;
the grader applies the committed predictor.

The benchmark is a research evaluation and makes no clinical-validity claim.
